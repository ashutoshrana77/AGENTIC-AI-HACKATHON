7 Most Beautiful Places of India

From Himalayan lakes to tropical beaches – discover India's most stunning destinations.

Kashmir Valley Ladakh Kerala Backwaters Goa Udaipur Hampi Andaman & Nicobar

India is home to incredibly diverse landscapes – snow-capped mountains, serene lakes, golden beaches, ancient ruins, and lush backwaters. This blog highlights seven of the most beautiful places that every traveler should experience at least once.

Jammu & Kashmir

Kashmir Valley

Known as "Paradise on Earth," the Kashmir Valley is famous for snow-capped peaks, serene lakes, and Mughal gardens.

Main Attractions

Dal Lake houseboats, Mughal Gardens (Shalimar, Nishat), Gulmarg meadows and gondola, Pahalgam valleys.

Best Time to Visit

March–August for pleasant weather; December–February for snow.

How to Reach

Fly to Srinagar; or train to Jammu Tawi then road to Srinagar.

Union Territory

Ladakh

A high-altitude trans-Himalayan region with dramatic deserts, deep blue lakes, and ancient monasteries.

Main Attractions

Pangong Tso Lake, Nubra Valley sand dunes, Leh Palace, Khardung La Pass, Hemis and Thiksey monasteries.

Best Time to Visit

May–September when roads and passes are open.

How to Reach

Fly to Leh; or road via Manali–Leh or Srinagar–Leh highway (seasonal).

Kerala

Kerala Backwaters

A network of lagoons, lakes, and canals along the Malabar Coast, lined with paddy fields and coconut groves.

Main Attractions

Alleppey houseboat cruises, Kumarakom bird sanctuary, Vembanad Lake, traditional villages and Kathakali shows.

Best Time to Visit

September–March (post-monsoon and winter).

How to Reach

Fly to Kochi or Trivandrum; train to Alappuzha/Kottayam; then local boats/road.

Goa

Goa

A coastal state known for golden beaches, Portuguese heritage, churches, forts, and a relaxed lifestyle.

Main Attractions

Baga, Calangute, Palolem beaches; Old Goa churches; Aguada and Chapora forts; Fontainhas Latin Quarter.

Best Time to Visit

November–February (cool, dry, ideal for beaches).

How to Reach

Fly to Goa (Dabolim/Manohar); train to Madgaon or Thivim; road from Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru.

Rajasthan

Udaipur

The "City of Lakes," famous for white marble palaces reflected in Lake Pichola and Aravalli hills.

Main Attractions

City Palace, Lake Pichola boat rides, Jag Mandir, Jagdish Temple, evening light & sound show.

Best Time to Visit

October–March (pleasant winter).

How to Reach

Fly to Udaipur; train to Udaipur City; road from Jaipur, Ahmedabad.

Karnataka

Hampi

UNESCO World Heritage Site with ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire set among giant boulders and paddy fields.

Main Attractions

Virupaksha Temple, Vittala Temple with stone chariot, Royal Centre, Hemakuta Hill sunset, Tungabhadra River coracle rides.

Best Time to Visit

October–February (cooler weather for exploring).

How to Reach

Nearest airports Hubballi/Belagavi then road; train to Hospet Junction then short drive to Hampi.

Andaman & Nicobar Islands

Andaman & Nicobar Islands

Tropical islands in the Bay of Bengal with white-sand beaches, turquoise water, coral reefs, and dense forests.

Main Attractions

Radhanagar Beach (Havelock), Cellular Jail (Port Blair), Ross Island ruins, Neil Island beaches, scuba diving and snorkeling.

Best Time to Visit

November–May (clear skies, good visibility for water sports).

How to Reach

Fly to Port Blair from major Indian cities; or passenger ships from Chennai, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam.
